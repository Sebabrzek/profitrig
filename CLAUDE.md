## ProfitRig brand and UI work

`docs/design/PROFITRIG_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` is the source of truth for ProfitRig's visual and UX design. Read it, and `docs/design/README.md`, before any frontend design or visual refactoring. Read `docs/design/CLAUDE_IMPLEMENTATION_START.md` before implementing design work.

Inspect the approved visual references under `docs/design/assets/` when doing UI work — the bison mark, the Option 01 wordmark reference, the component board, and the illustrations. Look at the images themselves, not just their filenames and descriptions.

The ProfitRig identity is decided, not open: the charging bison, the approved Option 01 wordmark direction, the approved palette, Satoshi for UI and Inter for body and JetBrains Mono for financial data, and the tagline KNOW YOUR NUMBERS. TAKE CONTROL. Do not redesign or reinterpret it. The `PENDING.md` files under `assets/logos/` mean the production vector files are still outstanding, not that the direction is unsettled. Historical screenshots in `assets/references/archive/` are not approved design targets.

Preserve existing business logic and financial calculations. Visual work must never change what a number says.

`docs/handoff.md` — how we work, what is live, and what is next — and `AGENTS.md` still govern the project workflow and the current development order.
